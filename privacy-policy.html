Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Bishal Imagery (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit bishalphotography.com or book a photography session with us, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that helps us respond to inquiries and provide our photography services.

Information you give us

  • Contact details — your name, email address, and phone number when you submit our contact form or reach out by email, phone, or WhatsApp.
  • Booking details — the type of session, event date, location, and any message or preferences you share with us.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — basic, non-identifying technical information such as browser type, device, and pages visited, which may be collected through standard analytics and our hosting provider.
  • Cookies — small files used to keep the site working properly. See Section 5.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and booking requests;
  • Plan, schedule, and deliver photography sessions and galleries;
  • Send you session-related communication, quotes, and invoices;
  • Improve our website and services; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

3. Photographs & Image Rights

Photographs taken during a session are handled according to the terms of your booking agreement. We will not publish or use your images for promotional purposes (such as our website or social media) without your consent, which you may grant or decline. You may request that we remove your images from our promotional channels at any time by contacting us.

4. How We Share Information

We share personal information only when necessary, and only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate (for example, email, scheduling, cloud gallery hosting, or payment processors), who are required to protect your information;
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features may not function as intended if you do.

6. Data Retention & Security

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting requirements. We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction or deletion of your information;
  • Withdraw consent for promotional use of your images; and
  • Opt out of non-essential communications.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. When children are photographed as part of a family or event session, this is done with the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites (such as Instagram or WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
